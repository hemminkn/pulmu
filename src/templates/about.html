about Pulmu...

welcome to Pulmu, my humble virtual magazine/blog/diary/personal project, even I'm not sure! you might be wondering (or not) where the name Pulmu comes from. it's actually a traditionally feminine given name in Finland. it comes from the name "pulmunen", a species of bird. to be honest I've never heard of anyone named Pulmu in Finland but I thought it was cute and sweet, like this project. ^^

I post about things I like and am interested in, like arts and crafts, things I'm up to, etc. I hope you enjoy this website! if you wish to, you can contact me! details in my contact page. :)
